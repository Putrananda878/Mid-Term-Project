About the abilities

Stamina Mastery

This item grants 2 extra stamina and an aditional air dash or air jump. Mina has low stamina so this item is mandatory

Quicksilver Reload

Replenishes her bulltets after using the imbued ability. Imbue it on Rake and Mina can immediately proc love bites on another enemy while the love bites are on cooldown

Superior Cooldown

Faster cooldown for love bites, rake, Nox Nostra, and sanguine retreat.

Ethereal Shift

Makes Mina invulnerable for a short duration. Allows Mina to play a lot more aggressively and gives her a jail out of free card. Use when caught and sanguine retreat is unavailable
