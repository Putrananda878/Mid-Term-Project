Mina Ha

Spirit burst, role 2, nimble, vampire, adn annoying

Strengths

Nimble

Mina's ability two offers both mobility and invincibility. With three stacks per each use and low cooldown, Mina becomes very had to catch and pin down

Quick farm

Mina's ability one works as an execute for lower health enemies, making her very effective at clearing tier 1 and 2 jungle camps. A good Mina can steal most of the enemy's jungle in a few seconds

Versatile

Mina's ult is an aoe that silences enemies. Used on many enemies, it can silence a whole team. Used for dueling, said opponent will be rendered helpless as all it's damage is foucused on bursting said enemy

Split pushing

Walkers have lower spirit resistance than buller resistance that lowers as the match progresses. Mina's high spirit damage can take a walker down in less than a minute. Her great mobility also ensures that a good Mina can escape easily

weaknesses

Low health

Mina has very low health and can be bursted down in seconds

low stamina

Mina only has two base stamina, requiring good stamina management and good movement from the player

Close ranged

Outside of Mina's ult, she has very low range and needs to be close to deal significant damage

Closed spaces

Mina's mobility is best used in open spaces as she can use them to run to higher ground. Her ult also requires her to be high in the air. A closed space denies both these things that are essential to her kit
