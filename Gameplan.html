Early Game

Mina is very strong in the early game. If the enemy doesn't take cover, love bites can whittle their health down. Her first skill can be used to proc love bites faster and to execute low health enemies. Sanguine Retreat allows her to immediately retreat after a trade, taking minimal losses. Rake can also be used to farm camps and ensures she is ahead in the economy, causing her to unlock ult first.

Mid Game

In the mid game, Mina's primary job is to steal enemy camps and help gank enemies. She also has a choice of either joining wars or simply splitpushing. Her ult ensures death to enemies that are off position or alone and also helps take down walkers faster. Sanguine retreat makes sure that when things go bad, either in war or splitpushing. Mina always has an escape

Late Game

By the late game, Mina's damage has fallen off significantly. She however is not useless. Rake and sanguine retreat ensures that Mina is still an effective kill confirm. Her ult(provided that Mina farmed enough bats) can also either silences the whole team for a long time or if Mina deems useful, burst down a single enemy if said enemy isn't a tank
